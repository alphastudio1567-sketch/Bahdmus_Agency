FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, process, and partnership.

How long does a typical project take? +
Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A standard website typically takes 4-6 weeks from discovery to launch. Complex projects with custom features may take 8-12 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance? +
Yes, we offer comprehensive maintenance packages that include security updates, performance optimization, content updates, and technical support. Maintenance plans start at $150/month and are tailored to your specific needs.
Can I update the website myself? +
Absolutely. We can integrate user-friendly content management systems (like a custom CMS or WordPress) that allow you to easily update text, images, and blog posts. We provide training and documentation for all CMS integrations.
What's included in your pricing? +
Our pricing includes discovery, design, development, testing, launch, and post-launch support. Each proposal details deliverables, timelines, and payment schedules. There are no hidden fees - everything is transparent from the start.
Do you work with international clients? +
Yes, we work with creative professionals worldwide. Our process is designed for remote collaboration, and we accommodate different time zones. We accept payments in multiple currencies and provide contracts in English.
