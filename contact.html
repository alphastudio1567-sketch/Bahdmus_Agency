FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to common questions about starting your project.

How long does the process take from inquiry to project start? +
Typically 1-3 business days. After your inquiry, we'll schedule a discovery call within 24 hours. Following the call, you'll receive a proposal within 1-2 business days. Once approved, we can start immediately based on our availability.
What information should I provide in my initial inquiry? +
For the best consultation, provide: 1) Project type (portfolio, e-commerce, etc.), 2) Timeline expectations, 3) Budget range, 4) Reference websites you like, 5) Any specific features or requirements. Don't worry if you're unsure—we'll guide you through everything.
Do you work with international clients? +
Yes! We work with creative professionals worldwide. Our process is designed for remote collaboration, and we accommodate different time zones. We accept payments in multiple currencies and provide clear contracts and communication throughout.
What happens after I submit my project inquiry? +
1. You'll receive confirmation within 24 hours. 2. We'll schedule a discovery call. 3. We'll create a custom proposal. 4. Once approved, we'll start with project planning and strategy. You'll have a dedicated point of contact throughout the entire process.
Can I see examples of similar projects you've done? +
Absolutely! Visit our portfolio page to see examples of artist portfolios, music websites, and creative brand sites. You can also request specific case studies during our consultation.
